# Dawnwager — Steam Early Access Roadmap

## Day-0 Early Access (shipping now)

- Offline Road to Ragnarök campaign (7 chapters)
- Tutorial crossing + skip
- Practice skirmishes with rotating modifiers
- Collection, deckbuilder (search + mana curve)
- Daily quests and Embers cosmetics
- Career stats and achievement unlocks
- Beacon Ladder UI with Casual / Ranked find-match
- Ranked-sim AI fill when population is low (clearly labeled)
- Windows SteamPipe packaging (see `README.md`)

## Milestone 1 — Live matchmaking

- Authoritative online duels wrapping `Match.Apply` / `Legal`
- Hidden Glicko MMR + star / floor ladder (Hearthstone public-spec parity)
- Casual and Ranked queues with expanding search radius
- Reconnect and concede handling
- Legend leaderboard (top 100)

## Milestone 2 — Live economy

- Seasonal ladder resets with star-bonus carry
- Expanded cosmetics (card backs, tables)
- Balance patches driven by ladder telemetry

## Positioning

Dawnwager is a **prophecy-first tactical CCG**: two lanes, contested Seals, and public omens. Competitive feel targets Hearthstone clarity without cloning Blizzard protocols.
