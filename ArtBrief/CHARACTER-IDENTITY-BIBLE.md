# Dawnwager character identity bible

This file records the visual anchors that must remain consistent between a card illustration and the played-board portrait. Names and details below apply only to these IDs; never transfer a face, mask, outfit, or distinctive prop to a different character.

| ID | Character identity | Card scene | Played pose |
|---|---|---|---|
| A01 — Bridge Squire | Iona: young adult woman, wheat-blond loose braid, freckles, grey-blue eyes, mustard wool mantle, slate padded coat, red round shield with brass boss. | She steps onto the icy bridge in a crosswind, shield low; the crossing and distant wardens establish scale. | She plants a boot and raises the shield to shelter a traveler behind her. |
| A02 — Goldsmith’s Apprentice | Lean young adult man, short copper hair, hazel eyes, soot smear across nose, faded teal scarf, rolled linen sleeves, stained brown leather apron. | Seated sideways at his bench, he shapes a tiny oath-ring with hammer and punch. | He leans toward the forge with tongs to lift the finished ring. |
| A05 — Hall Spear | Middle-aged dark-skinned man, square weathered face, salt hair, pale eyebrow scar, dull iron cap, deep pine-green wool and iron-reinforced coat. Carries one ash spear. | He braces side-on in a timber doorway, spear held horizontally to bar entry. | He pivots the spear upright and signals allies to halt. |
| A06 — Ringkeeper | Sovra: elderly man, narrow lined face, white beard, long silver hair in one low braid, ochre-brown wool work coat, slate apron, brass ring tools. | Crouched beside a scarred shield, he threads a brass oath-ring through a broken strap. | He raises the repaired shield in a grounded three-quarter pose. |
| A08 — Heimdall’s Lookout | The Site is a narrow watchtower built into the bridge end: iron-clad stone base, weathered timber roof, brass signal bell and a single lit beacon window. | Wide landscape view establishes the tower at the end of the frozen crossing. | Board portrait keeps the same tower, bell and beacon recognizable from a closer three-quarter angle. |
| A09 — Raven Standard | Tawny-skinned adult man, short ash-brown curls, broken nose, dark raven-feather mantle and tall black standard with forked raven finial. | He plants the raven standard between allied shields as a ward breaks. | He raises that same standard as a rally signal, upright in three-quarter view. |
| A10 — Hall Healer | Adult woman with warm brown skin, close-cropped black curls, one copper temple braid, slate healer’s coat, linen wraps and herb satchel. | Kneeling in profile, she lays a wrapped palm on an injured guard’s shoulder; amber healing light travels through the cloth. | She rises with one arm extended in a healing gesture and a folded bandage in the other hand. |
| A13 — Valkyrie Escort | Lean adult woman, dark auburn hair in two tight braids, weathered grey wool, bronze clasps and slate-blue cloak. | A17 — White-Hall Veteran | Elderly dark-skinned man, close silver curls, deep laugh lines, ivory-and-charcoal hall coat and worn oval iron shield. | He turns from a bench to strap on his shield as the prophecy bell sounds. | He stands listening toward the omen, shield slung securely behind him. |
| A19 — Hammer Retainer | Stocky middle-aged woman, warm brown skin, crown-wrapped black braid, cheek scar, soot-grey leather, rust-red sash and short broad-faced bronze maul. | She drives the maul into the snow beside an advancing foe. | She pivots into a defensive brace with the maul held low across her body. |
