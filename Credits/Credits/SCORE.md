# Original music for The Last Dawn

- **Menu** · 79 seconds. Tremolo strings and a slow falling refrain.
- **Campaign** · 69 seconds. Low choir, harp, and a walking cello line.
- **Battle** · 52 seconds. Repeated open fifths with a drum pulse.
- **Boss** · 46 seconds. Faster low strings, percussion and a harsher variation of the refrain.

Original compositions were rendered with FluidSynth and the TimGM6mb General MIDI soundfont. The soundfont is documented in the accompanying license file. The project ships rendered music. It does not include the soundfont or synthesizer.
