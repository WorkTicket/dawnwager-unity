# WOLFSUN
*The Last Dawn · First-set design bible · Playtest version 0.1*

**The wolf ate the sun. What are you willing to bring back?**

WOLFSUN is a two-player tactical card game set during an alternate Ragnarök. The disasters that will hit the board are visible from the opening hand. Build a plan around them, force the other player to commit before they land, and fight over the routes that still connect the living world.

## The night Odin opened the gate

The skalds sang that Odin would die when Fenrir broke his chains. When Odin stopped correcting their song, the skalds took it for courage. Then he started asking what happened to the people who never made it into the verse.

The last page of his prophecy held the answer. The old gods had a way to survive Ragnarök. They could pay for a new sun with every name in Midgard. Its dead would leave no ghosts. Its children would leave no empty beds. Even Hel would have nothing to gather.

Odin opened Fenrir’s gate himself. He thought a wolf freed by choice could break a fate built on betrayal. Fenrir ran straight past him, crossed the sky, and swallowed the sun. At the horizon, there was a brief second sunrise from inside his throat.

That is where the match begins. Heimdall’s beacons can still draw light from either end of the Bifröst. Refugees, giants and gods fight over the crossings. Meanwhile the omens keep coming. The world knows when the next one will arrive. For the first time, the prophecy cannot say who will survive it.

### The first set in four scenes

1. **The Open Gate.** Týr watches Odin remove the last lock. Fenrir asks him to come along. Týr stays to warn the settlements. Odin spends the rest of the set unable to tell anyone why he opened the gate without exposing his own part in the original bargain.
2. **Winter at the Ferry.** Runa, a human ferry captain, finds that her longship can sail on water frozen by Fimbulwinter. She ties it to a beacon and runs passengers across by the hundreds. Thor arrives to reclaim the crossing. Runa gives him an oar.
3. **The House of Names.** Hel shelters the refugees that die trying to cross. Loki steals the page that named Midgard as payment. He reads it aloud at the western beacon and changes which army is willing to hold the bridge.
4. **The Last Dawn.** Thor sees Fenrir sleeping under the eastern pier. The last sunlight is shining out through a wound in the wolf’s side. A girl is warming her hands over it. Thor puts his hammer on the ground. Behind him, Surtr asks whether anyone has thought to warn the wolf that the child is there.

In the closing scene, the girl catches a drop of light in Runa’s lantern. Runa’s ferry takes it across the river. Asgard wants to rebuild the sun. Hel wants to keep it somewhere no throne can reach. The wolf still carries the rest.

**The cast.** Thor and Týr anchor Asgard. Runa and Eydis are original human protagonists. Fenrir fights alongside the giants, though he owes them no oath. Surtr commands their late-game threats. Hel and Garmr guard the dead. Loki and Angrboða know parts of the prophecy Odin never read. The story is an original retelling informed by the [Poetic Edda](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/73533) and [Prose Edda](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/18947). Use original artwork and characterization for this version. WOLFSUN is a working title.

## Match rules

### Setup and victory

- Start at 24 Bastion health, zero Seals, and zero maximum Charge. Build exactly 30 cards from up to two factions and Neutral. Up to two of each standard card and one of each Legendary. Tokens and prophecies are outside decks.
- Reveal two different shared prophecies, chosen uniformly from the six listed below. Assign the first drawn to round 3 and the second to round 5. Prophecies are public before mulligans. For a first match, use Fimbulwinter in round 3 and The Sun Goes Out in round 5.
- Draw four, privately set aside any number, draw replacements, and shuffle the set-aside cards back. Randomly choose the first player. First player alternates each round.
- Two lanes, West and East. Each player has three Unit slots and one separate Site slot per lane. From round 2 onward West is contested on even rounds and East on odd rounds.
- Win by reducing the enemy Bastion to zero or collecting six Seals. To score at round end, have at least one allied unit in the contested lane and a strictly higher sum of current Attack there than the opponent, including exhausted units. Apply scoring-only modifiers. A tie awards neither player a Seal. Round 7 is the earliest possible Seal win. If both Bastions reach zero in the same event, the game is a draw.

### Round sequence

1. **Refresh.** Increase each player's maximum Charge by one, up to 10. Refill Charge and ready units. Resolve round-start abilities, then both players draw one card from round 2 onward.
2. **Actions.** Alternate one action each. Play a card, attack with one unit, march one unit, or pass. Finish an action and its triggers before the next action. Two consecutive passes end actions. An action other than passing breaks the chain. No cards are played as reactions during another action.
3. **Prophecy.** Resolve all prophecies due this round in original reveal order, draining the trigger queue after each prophecy.
4. **Round end.** Resolve end-of-round abilities, score the contested lane, then expire this round’s effects. Unspent Charge is replaced at next refresh.

### Actions and card types

- **Units** enter an open allied Unit slot. They enter exhausted unless they have Rush. A ready unit may attack and exhaust itself, dealing its Attack to an enemy in its own lane while the enemy deals its Attack back simultaneously. If there are no enemy units in that lane, it may attack the enemy Bastion, which deals no damage back. If any enemy in the lane has Guard, the attacker must choose a Guard. Combat damage remains on units.
- **March** moves a ready unit to an open allied slot in the other lane and exhausts it. Card-based movement preserves readiness and also needs an open slot on the unit’s controller’s side. A swap exchanges two units controlled by the same player in different lanes simultaneously, even if both lanes are full.
- **Orders** resolve, enter discard, then trigger abilities that listen for playing an Order. Choose targets, modes and any lane at play. Mandatory targets must be legal. **Sites** enter an empty allied Site slot. Sites persist, cannot attack or be attacked, and can be destroyed by card effects. There is no default Site replacement action.

### Shared terms and timing

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Deploy | Triggers when a Unit or Site enters after being played from hand. |
| Farewell | Triggers when a unit dies. Recall is separate from death. |
| Rush | Enters ready. It cannot attack a Bastion in the round it entered, even when readied again. |
| Guard | Restricts enemy attacks in the same lane. Orders and abilities ignore Guard. |
| Shield | Prevents the next positive damage instance to this unit and is then removed. Does not stack. |
| Summon | Create a named token in an open allied Unit slot. It enters exhausted unless it has Rush. If full, skip. Multiple summons fill slots one by one in written order. |
| Revive | Return a Unit card that died under your control and is still in your discard pile. It resets to base stats and has normal entry readiness. Summon and Revive do not trigger Deploy. Leaving the discard pile ends eligibility until it dies again. |
| Recall | Return a unit to its owner’s hand as a clean card. A recalled token disappears. |
| Forfeit N | Deal N damage to your own Bastion as the first instruction of the ability. Check for defeat before continuing. |
| Unchain N | This card in hand costs 1 less per Seal the opponent already has, up to N less. |
| Omenbound | A triggered ability that queues after each prophecy resolves, including one that causes no damage. Only units or Sites still in play at that point trigger. |

- A unit may be readied by an effect during its entry round only if it has Rush. A ready unit cannot become doubly ready. “Ready an exhausted unit” excludes already-ready units. A killed or recalled unit chosen earlier does not return with its previous marks, buffs or damage.
- Simultaneous damage and simultaneous destruction affect all listed targets in one event. Remove all units at zero health together after each event. A shield only prevents damage. Dead cards enter their owner’s discard pile, and dead tokens disappear after their death is recorded. Turn off effects from removed units immediately. Attacks, deaths, Seal totals and schedule shifts are public. A Bastion can heal up to 24. Check for defeat after every atomic event.
- Abilities triggered together enter a first-in, first-out queue. The round’s first player orders their triggers, then the other player orders theirs. At prophecy resolution queue death and any Shield-loss triggers before queuing Omenbound. “Died to this prophecy” counts units killed directly by its effect and excludes deaths caused later by triggers. Existing triggers finish resolving in queue order. A queued ability can finish after its source leaves play. Choose targets for triggered abilities at resolution. Skip an instruction with a missing target and skip any dependent instruction. “If you do” requires the previous action to succeed.
- “Here” is the source’s lane at resolution, or its last lane when it has left play. “Other” excludes the source. When no lane is specified for a summon or revive, choose a legal allied lane. An effect that moves an exact number may choose fewer only if its text says “up to.” If an effect tells both players to choose, they make choices openly in round initiative order before the effect applies. When each player must perform separate draw or summon instructions, do so in initiative order. Simultaneous instructions say so explicitly. Unless tied to a particular player by wording, choices for shared prophecies use that same order.
- Stat buffs last while the unit remains on the board unless given a duration. +Health raises current and maximum by the same amount. Attack and Charge costs have a floor of zero. Any “printed cost” check uses the catalog cost. Unit tokens count as printed cost zero for these checks. Charge gained cannot exceed maximum Charge.
- Hand limit is eight. Overflow is revealed and discarded. The first failure to draw from an empty deck deals 1 damage to your Bastion, the second 2, and so on. Simultaneous refresh draw failures apply together. Looking at or revealing the top of an empty deck does nothing. Use as many cards as are present when instructed to look at a number of cards. A player chooses their own discards when told to discard.
- **Shifting the schedule.** Each prophecy can shift at most once. A shift may advance or delay it by one round in the inclusive range of rounds 2–6, and cannot place it before the current round. Resolve multiple prophecies due together in original reveal order. “Next prophecy” means the earliest pending prophecy, with original reveal order breaking ties. If it cannot be shifted, skip that instruction. A shift affects both players.

## Shared prophecies

| ID | Prophecy | Full effect |
|---|---|---|
| P01 | Fimbulwinter | Deal 2 damage to every unit. |
| P02 | The Sun Goes Out | Exhaust all units. For the rest of this round, exhausted units contribute 1 less Attack each to Seal scoring, to a minimum of zero per unit. |
| P03 | The Sea Walks Inland | Each player with at least one unit in the contested lane and space in their other lane chooses one of their units in the contested lane. Move the chosen units to the other lane simultaneously. |
| P04 | The Dead Knock | Each player summons a Restless Soul in their own contested lane. |
| P05 | Bifröst Breaks | Deal 3 damage to units in the contested lane and 1 damage to units in the other lane, simultaneously. |
| P06 | The Gates Burn | Destroy all Sites simultaneously. Each player with no Sites immediately before the destruction draws a card in initiative order. |

**Example.** Fimbulwinter is due in round 3. You play a shielded defender and your opponent builds a lane of units with Farewell. After you both pass, winter consumes your defender’s Shield and kills two of your opponent’s units. First resolve Shield-loss and Farewell abilities in queue order, then surviving Omenbound units resolve theirs. Compare Attack in the contested lane after those effects. If Loki shifted winter to round 4, the confrontation happens at the other beacon instead.

## First playtest

**Start with The Lantern Guard versus No One Left Behind.** Use Fimbulwinter on round 3 and The Sun Goes Out on round 5. This pairing puts Shields against Farewell and gives each player a reason to fight for both lanes. Then try Wrong Side of the River versus The Burial Avalanche. That matchup tests whether informed repositioning can beat a deck willing to lose its own board.

### A round you can set up and play

It’s round 3. East is contested. Fimbulwinter is due after the action phase. Asgard has a healthy, ready 2/2 Ringkeeper in East. Hel’s Court has an exhausted 1/2 Grave Candle Bearer and a healthy, exhausted 2/4 Bone Listener there. Both players have enough Charge for the cards below.

1. Asgard plays **Raise the Shield** on Ringkeeper.
2. Hel plays **Knock Once** on Grave Candle Bearer, setting up a second death trigger.
3. Asgard and Hel pass consecutively. Fimbulwinter deals 2 to all units.
4. Ringkeeper’s Shield absorbs the damage. The Candle Bearer dies. The Listener survives at 2 health. Ringkeeper grows to 3/3. The Candle Bearer and Knock Once each summon a 1/1 Restless Soul into the two open slots. Bone Listener gains 1 Attack.
5. East scores **5–3** for Hel’s Court. Asgard kept its unit through the wipe and can fight back next round. The Seal went to the player who prepared for the damage.

Try the same position with **Change the Subject** available to Asgard instead of Raise the Shield, using the Asgard/Outcasts deck. Delay Fimbulwinter to round 4. Grave Candle Bearer survives, Knock Once expires this round, and the contested lane shifts to West before winter arrives. Asgard still trails 2–3 in East and needs another action to take the current Seal.

### What to measure

| Design question | Record during playtests |
|---|---|
| Did forecasted events create decisions? | For each player, note a play, attack or placement that changed because the next prophecy was visible. |
| Did shifting feel interactive? | Record when it moved, what the opponent had already committed, and whether the new timing gave either player a line they could play around. |
| Do both win routes matter? | Record winner, win route, round, final health and final Seals. Check whether the losing player had a credible alternate route. |
| Do comeback discounts reward falling behind too much? | Record when Unchain cards first hit the board, how many Seals the opponent had, and whether earlier Seal gains still mattered. |
| Did both decks get to express their identity? | Record cards stuck in hand, reasons, and the first recognizable play the deck made. |

The prototype has starting values for cost, stats and win thresholds. The 30-card lists have passed structural checks. Match length, matchup balance and whether players can plan around schedule shifts are open playtest questions.
