# The Road to Ragnarök

Dawnwager tells an original story about keeping the crossings open. The seven encounters adapt episodes from the Eddas. Runa, the ferry trials, the duels, the map's geography, and the player’s role in the events are inventions for the game.

- [Gylfaginning 49](https://openedda.is/gylfaginning/49/) recounts Hermóðr’s ride to Hel and the attempt to release Baldr. The Hel chapter has the characters revisit that story.
- [Gylfaginning 51](https://northerntales.se/en/eddas/prose-edda/gylfaginning/51/) covers Fimbulwinter, the breaking of the bonds, Naglfar, Heimdall’s horn, the battle at Vígríðr and Surtr’s fire. The campaign visits these events in a playable route.
- [The Eddas and the world renewed](https://openedda.is/mimir/story/) covers the green earth, Baldr's return, and the survival of Líf and Lífþrasir. These images close the campaign.
