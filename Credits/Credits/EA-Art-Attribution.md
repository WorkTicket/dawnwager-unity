## Kenney · Fantasy UI Borders

- Artist and source: [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets/fantasy-ui-borders).
- License: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- Used as resized and re-colored border geometry in Panel, PanelGold, ModalFrame, Tooltip, SeasonBadge, NavBar, BannerFrame, LadderPlate, SearchField and MatchFound.

The Dawnwager emblem, title and mode illustrations, and deck back art are project assets. The rank-frame hardware, End Turn lantern, and prophecy frame were generated for this remaster and composited into the production sprites. The Legend medal, first_seal achievement and Seal discs use the game's existing bitten-sun emblem.
