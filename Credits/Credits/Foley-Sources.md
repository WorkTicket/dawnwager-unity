# Dawnwager foley sources

The new WAV effects were assembled from recorded sources using timing, light pitch shifting, EQ, layered impact and controlled peaks. The audition reel contains selected installed cues. No oscillator tones were added to the new effects.

| Source | License | Use |
|---|---|---|
| [Kenney, 50 RPG sound effects](https://opengameart.org/content/50-rpg-sound-effects) | CC0 1.0 | Book, card-like paper, leather, mechanical latches, coins, tools and impacts |
| [Till Behrend, Glass Break](https://opengameart.org/content/glass-break) | CC0 1.0 | Shield shatter, winter crack and prophecy texture |
| [AntumDeluge, Fire Crackling](https://opengameart.org/content/fire-crackling) | CC0 1.0 | Boss introduction bed |

The four shipped score tracks and the 160 existing card performance cues retain their original credits. See SCORE.md and RECORDING-CREDITS.md.
